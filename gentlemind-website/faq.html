What services does GentleMind provide?

+

GentleMind provides comprehensive technology solutions including custom software development, mobile app development, cloud solutions, cybersecurity services, AI and machine learning solutions, and digital transformation consulting. We serve clients across various industries with tailored solutions to meet their specific needs.

How long has GentleMind been in business?

+

GentleMind has been providing technology solutions for over 15 years. We have successfully delivered 500+ projects for 200+ clients worldwide, establishing ourselves as a trusted partner in the technology industry.

Yes, we offer comprehensive support and maintenance services including 24/7 monitoring, bug fixes, security updates, performance optimization, and feature enhancements. We provide flexible support packages tailored to your needs.

Our pricing is based on project scope, complexity, timeline, and specific requirements.
